Outcast Uplink

Casts whatever a Volumio player plays out to any uplink: Icecast and SHOUTcast servers, RTMP and SRT ingests, transport streams, HLS and DASH, a stream on the network, a recording. Every service the player has is carried, the title the player shows goes with it, and the player never waits on an uplink. DJ mode makes the programme and puts jingles and inserts into the stream.

The evo framework where this comes from

Outcast Uplink is part of the evo framework, and grew out of the work on evo-device-audio: how a player's sound can be taken from its own audio chain without touching it, measured, and carried on. Every name beginning with evo, evotap, evopeg and the rest, is a piece of that framework, direct or derived.

Scan the code, or go to evoframework.org.
